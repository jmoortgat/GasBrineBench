# Sources

**Every number in this benchmark was measured by someone else.** We hold
the primary papers locally in order to transcribe them; we do not and
cannot redistribute them. This manifest is how that circle is squared:
for each source it gives the full bibliographic reference, a DOI, a
resolvable URL, and exactly which rows of the benchmark came from it.
A reader can therefore obtain each paper through their own library and
reconstruct the entire database from primary sources -- without us
redistributing a single copyrighted page.
